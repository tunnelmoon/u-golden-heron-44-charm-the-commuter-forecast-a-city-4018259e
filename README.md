# Charm the Commuter: Forecast a City That Isn't

Entry for the make.fun bounty [Charm the Commuter: Forecast a City That Isn't](https://make.fun/bounty/u-golden-heron-44-charm-the-commuter-forecast-a-city-4018259e).

One line of weather, 140 characters at most, for a city that does not exist: Snozzlewick, where confetti drizzles until 9, then sunshine and a free second coffee for anyone still waiting at the bus stop.

`main.ts` validates the line (1 to 140 characters, one line, not only whitespace) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
